PERSISTENT LIVE MUSIC

The Live Music player now stays mounted while users move between the store pages using normal internal links. Navigation is handled without a full page reload, so the YouTube iframe is preserved and the song does not restart on each page.

For links triggered by JavaScript, use window.ytNavigate("page.html") instead of location.href so the player remains alive.

Browser/app termination, a hard refresh, opening a new tab, or browser autoplay restrictions can still stop/restart playback; those are browser limitations.
