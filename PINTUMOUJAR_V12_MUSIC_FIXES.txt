PINTUMOUJAR V12 MUSIC FIXES

1. YouTube redirect protection:
   - Embedded YouTube iframe is non-clickable.
   - Site overlay/custom controls handle play/pause so taps do not open YouTube.

2. Persistent music across Admin -> Store and internal website navigation:
   - Admin music iframe can be transferred to the storefront without destroying it.
   - Playback position is stored in localStorage and restored after page refresh.

3. Admin music state survives refresh:
   - Current live song remains loaded from the server.
   - Last YouTube search query/results are cached locally and restored, so you do not need to search again after refresh.

4. Visitor music:
   - Public pages keep the live music player.
   - Autoplay is attempted, but browsers can still require the visitor to tap Listen/Play.

5. Double playback prevention remains enabled.
